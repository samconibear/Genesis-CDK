How is this calculated?

Under the World Handicap System (WHS) used by England Golf, your Score Differential measures how well you played relative to a scratch golfer on that specific course and set of tees.

Score Differential
(113 ÷ Slope Rating) × (Adjusted Gross Score − Course Rating)
Course Handicap
Handicap Index × (Slope Rating ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par) (rounded)
Net Score
Adjusted Gross Score − Course Handicap

The Slope Rating (55–155, standard 113) reflects how much harder a course is for a bogey golfer vs a scratch golfer. The Course Rating is the expected score for a scratch golfer. Both are set by England Golf for each set of tees.

Course data is sourced from published England Golf ratings. Always confirm current tee ratings with your club before submitting a score to your handicapping authority.
